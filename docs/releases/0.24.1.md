Version 0.24.1 is the first public release of Heat Tile Painter for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5.

Paint heat-tile patterns or flat colors directly onto parts in the VAB and SPH. Each part keeps an
ordered layer stack with controls for side, coverage, surface wrap, mirror, edge style, placement,
rotation, and visibility. Paint remains attached through save and load, launch, and revert.

The renderer uses mesh-based material regions to keep paint off the opposite side of models that
share UV space. It does not leave decal parts or persistent overlay renderers on the craft. One
global scheduler limits restoration and repaint to one painted part per frame.

Functional heat protection is optional. It adds 5% dry mass in exchange for 35% higher internal and
50% higher skin temperature limits, capped at 3000 K and 3300 K.

## Requirements

- Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5
- ModuleManager

## Install

Copy the archive's `GameData` folder into the Kerbal Space Program folder and merge it with the
existing `GameData` folder. The mod installs to `GameData/HeatTileOverlayPrototype`.

## Compatibility

Reforged Materials Redux is not required. ReStockPBR, Textures Unlimited, Shabby, Resurfaced,
Technicolor, and Conformal Decals are also not required.

## Current limits

- Mask opacity is saved, but the renderer does not interpret it yet.
- Textured gasket and Reinforced strip currently use the same smooth boundary as Soft fade.
- Some complex multi-material parts remain limited by their source UV layout.
- The first use of an uncached texture or region can pause the editor while it is baked.
