Version 0.25.0 adds adjustable heat protection, paint that stays on deployable covers, and a
custom texture library, with fixes from the rendering and lifecycle audit.

Optional coating now ranges from 0.25x to 4x. The panel shows the resulting temperature limits and
mass cost: +1.25% to +20% dry mass, with caps reaching 6000 K internal / 6600 K skin. Existing enabled
craft keep 1x. Higher original limits and later changes from other modules are preserved.

Stock aircraft landing gear and stock/ReStock shielded docking ports and shrouded solar panels
keep paint on their exterior covers as they move. Wheels, mechanisms, inner cover faces, and
solar cells stay original. Unknown deployable models remain unpainted.

Patterns are now projected from the settled part-local surface instead of inheriting each stock UV
atlas's scale and origin. That keeps the physical size and phase consistent across renderer and
material boundaries, including distorted nose and intake UVs. Each layer also has a saved Pattern
size control from 0.25x to 4x.

Browse / Add custom imports PNG and JPG files into a persistent local library without storing the
original PC path in the craft. Pack authors can use the documented `HEAT_TILE_TEXTURE` config format
for arbitrary GameData textures, normal maps, physical span, and finish values.

The update also preserves missing texture IDs, fixes low-opacity interpretation and cache identity,
checks import dimensions before decoding, restores original meshes on clear, and keeps conditional
paint controls stable across Unity's Layout and Repaint events.
Procedural resizing follows the generator's mesh, and editor undo supports stock timing and the
KSP Community Fixes undo patch.

Repeated UVs, zero-area UV faces, and some complex multi-material models retain mapping limitations. Uncached bakes
can pause the editor; overlapping atlas regions require extra material batches. The legacy Mask
opacity switch is disabled, and the gasket/reinforced choices are labeled as fades.

Verified in KSP 1.12.5 with inspected editor/flight screenshots and instrumented lifecycle checks,
including the retained nose/intake regressions. See the [verification scope](https://github.com/peachyGT/HeatTilePainter/blob/v0.25.0/docs/VERIFICATION-0.25.0.md)
for results and limitations.
