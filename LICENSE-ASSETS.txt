The patterned HRSI textures in Heat Tile Painter are derived from the B9 Procedural Wings
`wing_surface_layered.dds` artwork. `hrsi_b9_hd` is a faithful 2x upscale of the
atlas-derived `hrsi_b9_exact`; it introduces no new panel artwork.

The gasket and reinforced-strip edge textures are original procedurally generated
materials made for Heat Tile Painter and released under the project's MIT License.
